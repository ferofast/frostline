# FROSTLINE — freeze is the only wall

[![reward](https://make.fun/badge/best-game-01/ferofast/frostline/reward.svg)](https://make.fun/bounty/best-game-01)

A mountain village below a volcano. Ember imps march down the one meltwater river
toward the village, and you are the Frost Warden. **Freeze is the only wall.**

Click a river segment to freeze it solid: frozen water blocks the embers and
shatters them — but frozen water does not flow, and flowing water is what feeds
the mill and earns Frost. Every click is a trade-off: fight, or earn.

- **WIN:** clear wave 10 and break the Ember Colossus.
- **LOSE:** village HEAT reaches 0.
- 10 waves, 3 enemy types + the Colossus boss, 6 frost relics (pick 1 of 3
  between waves), 3 routes, endless mode after victory.
- Enemies walk **in** the river — there are no lanes and no towers. A frozen
  segment is a temporary wall: it melts, and the Colossus melts it faster.

Made for the [make.fun best-game-01 bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/best-game-01).
Plays offline in a desktop browser: open `game/index.html`. No network, no
libraries — plain 2D canvas.

## Controls

- **Click a river segment** — freeze it (1 Frost) / thaw it early.
- **1 / 2 / 3** — pick a relic between waves.
- **Space** — start / pause. **R** — restart after victory or defeat. **M** — sound.
- `?shot=cover|title|relic|victory&seed=N` renders a fixed scene for screenshots.
